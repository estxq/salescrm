Privacy Policy

Schedule Hub · Last updated: 25 September 2026

Schedule Hub is a small scheduling tool for a caller and an agent working together as a team. This page explains what information it handles.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and a hashed password (we never store your password in readable form).
  • Your team's work: contacts (names, phone numbers, optional email and notes), meeting times, meeting outcomes and notifications that you or your teammate enter.
  • Zoom (if you connect it): a connection token that lets the app create, move and delete meetings on your Zoom account, and read the meetings already on it so they show in your calendar.
  • Google Calendar (if you connect it): a read-only connection token, used to show your calendar events to your teammate the Caller. We cannot create, edit or delete Google Calendar events.

How it is used

Only to provide the features above to the two people in your team. Information belongs to your team and is not visible to any other team. We do not sell it, share it for advertising, or use it to train any models.

Where it is stored

On our hosting provider (Vercel) and database provider (Upstash). Connection tokens are stored only so the app can act for you until you disconnect.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Zoom or Google Calendar at any time in the app, which removes our stored token. You can also revoke access from your Zoom or Google account settings.
  • Delete your account from the account menu. Team data such as contacts stays with the remaining teammate; ask us if you want it erased entirely.
